11. Best-practice notes for this page
- Always scope CTA/text locators under the comparison section or tile, not the entire page.
- Do not assume `section` is the root; use the observed live DOM and fallback to `main` as a safe parent.
- Use regex tolerance for superscript artifacts and punctuation, for example `/That'?s \$350 in Benefits!?/i`.
- Do not assert on a broad page-level text match when the same string may exist in multiple cards or sections.
- For unstable sessions, retry navigation, clear cookies/session as needed, and fail with an explicit challenge-page diagnostic before asserting locators.

12. Acceptance criteria mapping to live DOM
- AC1: comparison block visible with heading “Score the Right Membership for You”

- AC8: ScoreCard side includes `ScoreCard Logo Light None`, plus reward copy `1 Point For Every $1 Spent.` and `300 Points = $10 Reward.`
- AC10: benefits text should be validated with regex tolerance, not exact literal match on the full string, because the DOM includes superscript markers.
